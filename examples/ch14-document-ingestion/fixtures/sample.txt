# Quarterly Financial Report

Author: Jane Smith
Date: January 15, 2024

## Executive Summary

The company achieved strong results this quarter, with revenue reaching
$4.2 million, up 15% from the previous quarter. Operating expenses were
held to $2.8 million, resulting in operating income of $1.4 million.

## Revenue Breakdown

### Product Sales

Product sales totaled $3.1 million, driven primarily by growth in the
enterprise segment. Key highlights:

- Enterprise licenses: $2.0 million
- SMB subscriptions: $0.8 million
- Consumer products: $0.3 million

### Services Revenue

Professional services contributed $1.1 million, with consulting
engagements accounting for 70% of the total.

## Operating Expenses

### Personnel

Salaries and benefits totaled $1.8 million, representing 64% of
operating expenses. Headcount remained stable at 45 employees.

### Infrastructure

Cloud and infrastructure costs were $0.6 million, down 10% due to
optimization efforts completed in December.

### Marketing

Marketing spend was $0.4 million, focused primarily on digital
campaigns and trade show participation.

## Outlook

We expect continued growth in Q2, with revenue projected at $4.5-4.8
million. Key initiatives include:

1. Launch of new enterprise tier
2. Expansion into European markets
3. Enhanced partner program

The board approved a $500,000 investment in R&D for next quarter.
